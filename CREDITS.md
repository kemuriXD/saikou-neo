# Credits

Saikou Neo gets its information, artwork and ratings from the services below.
None of them make or endorse the app.

## Anime information

- [AniList](https://anilist.co): titles, synopses, episode counts, seasons,
  scores, related anime and recommendations.
- [AniZip](https://ani.zip): episode titles, air dates and stills, and the ids
  that link a show across the other sites.

## Artwork

- [TMDB](https://www.themoviedb.org): title logos and backgrounds. This product
  uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
- [fanart.tv](https://fanart.tv): title logos and backgrounds.
- [TheTVDB](https://thetvdb.com), through AniZip: fan art and episode stills.

## Ratings

- AniList: its own average score.
- [MyAnimeList](https://myanimelist.net): scores through
  [Tenrai](https://api.tenrai.org), an unofficial mirror of MyAnimeList's public
  catalogue.
- IMDb and Rotten Tomatoes: ratings through [OMDb](https://www.omdbapi.com).
  OMDb's data is licensed under
  [CC BY-NC 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/).

## Logos

The AniList, MyAnimeList, IMDb and Rotten Tomatoes logos on the Details screen
are trademarks of their owners. They're shown only to say where each rating
comes from.
